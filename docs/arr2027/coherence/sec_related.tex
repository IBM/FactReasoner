\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
%% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Factuality and consistency.} Claim-level metrics aggregate independent per-claim
verdicts \citep{factscore,safe,veriscore}, over decompositions whose choice matters
\citep{wanner2024}, and FactReasoner \citep{factreasoner} replaces counting with inference over
claims and evidence; \citet{lorefact} show that such pipelines miss errors in the logic that
connects the claims. Consistency metrics check a text against a source \citep{factcc,summac,alignscore,minicheck} or flag inconsistencies across samples, within a generation or dialogue, and across a model's beliefs \citep{selfcheckgpt,mundler2024,welleck2019,decode,ribeiro2019,li2019logic,elazar2021,becel}; reasoning-chain metrics score explicit derivations \citep{roscoe,receval,entailmentbank,lightman2024}. None of them says
how supports and conflicts should combine into one score, and none fixes a committed world under
which refuting a falsehood counts as support.

\paragraph{Discourse coherence} rests on cohesive ties, entity continuity or inferred relations
\citep{hallidayhasan1976,grosz1995,hobbs1979,mann,pdtb} and is scored by entity grids, neural models,
DiscoScore, entity graphs and LLM judges \citep{barzilay,li,discoscore,liustrube,geval,zheng2023judge}
against human ratings \citep{gcdc}, without regard to the truth of the claims.

\paragraph{Argumentation and logical constraints.} Argument mining recovers support and attack relations \citep{stab2017}, quality is rated for cogency and persuasiveness \citep{wachsmuth2017,habernal2016,persing2015}, and entailment graphs link propositions \citep{berant2011}. Gradual semantics for bipolar argumentation grade arguments under axioms such
as neutrality and monotony \citep{cayrol2005,amgoud2016,amgoud2018,baroni2019}, the closest
precedent to our axioms, but they grade acceptability from attacks and supports, not accordance
with a committed truth assignment. Probabilistic argumentation and explanatory coherence constrain beliefs
\citep{thimm2012,hunter2013,thagard1989,thagard1998}, and BeliefBank, ConCoRD and Maieutic prompting
repair them with weighted MAX-SAT or factor graphs \citep{beliefbank,concord,maieutic}, as in Markov
logic and PSL \citep{mln,psl}; we use such a model to \emph{measure} a response.

\paragraph{Probabilistic coherence and confirmation.} Bayesian epistemology measures the coherence of
a set of propositions under a joint distribution
\citep{shogenji1999,olsson2002,fitelson2003,douven2007}, with impossibility results on
truth-conduciveness \citep{bovens2003}. Confirmation is measured by the log-ratio
\citep{milne1996} or the weight of evidence \citep{good1950}, which are not ordinally equivalent
\citep{fitelson1999,crupi2007}; $\LCS$ is the logistic of the weight of evidence for the committed
world, and unlike coherence measures it grades the relations against truth values
(App.~\ref{app:verify}).
