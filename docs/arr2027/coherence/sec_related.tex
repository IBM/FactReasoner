\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
%% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Factuality and consistency.} Claim-level metrics aggregate independent per-claim
verdicts \citep{factscore,safe,veriscore}, and FactReasoner \citep{factreasoner} replaces counting
with inference over claims and evidence; \citet{lorefact} identify the resulting logic gap. NLI-based detectors flag inconsistencies with a source
\citep{summac}, across samples \citep{selfcheckgpt}, within one generation \citep{mundler2024} or across a model's beliefs \citep{elazar2021},
and reasoning-chain metrics score explicit derivations \citep{roscoe,receval,entailmentbank}. None says how
supports and conflicts combine, or has a committed world under which refuting a falsehood counts as
support.

\paragraph{Discourse coherence.} It is explained by cohesive ties \citep{hallidayhasan1976}, entity
continuity \citep{grosz1995} or inferred relations \citep{hobbs1979,mann,pdtb}, and scored by entity
grids \citep{barzilay}, neural models \citep{li}, DiscoScore \citep{discoscore}, entity--discourse
graphs \citep{liustrube} and LLM judges \citep{geval,zheng2023judge}, none with a guarantee on how
the score responds to the truth constraints between claims.

\paragraph{Argumentation and logical constraints.} Argument mining recovers support and attack
relations \citep{stab2017}, and argument quality is rated for cogency \citep{wachsmuth2017}. Gradual semantics for bipolar argumentation grade arguments under
axioms such as neutrality and monotony \citep{cayrol2005,amgoud2016,amgoud2018,baroni2019}, the
closest precedent to our axioms, and probabilistic argumentation and explanatory coherence constrain
beliefs by attacks or by positive and negative constraints
\citep{thimm2012,hunter2013,thagard1989,thagard1998}. BeliefBank \citep{beliefbank}, ConCoRD \citep{concord}
and Maieutic prompting \citep{maieutic} use weighted MAX-SAT or factor graphs over NLI constraints
to make beliefs consistent, as in Markov logic \citep{mln} and PSL \citep{psl}. We use such a model
to \emph{measure} a response, which raises a question they do not face: which readout is
monotone.

\paragraph{Probabilistic coherence and confirmation.} Bayesian epistemology measures the coherence of
a set of propositions under a joint distribution
\citep{shogenji1999,olsson2002,fitelson2003,douven2007}, with impossibility results on
truth-conduciveness \citep{bovens2003}. Confirmation theory measures how evidence moves belief in a
hypothesis, by the log-ratio \citep{milne1996} or the weight of evidence \citep{good1950}, which are
not ordinally equivalent in general \citep{fitelson1999,crupi2007}. $\LCS$ is a transform of the
weight of evidence for the committed world; unlike coherence measures, it holds the propositions
fixed and varies the relations.
