\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
%% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Factuality and consistency.} Claim-level metrics aggregate independent per-claim verdicts
\citep{factscore,safe,veriscore}, over decompositions whose choice matters \citep{wanner2024};
FactReasoner \citep{factreasoner} reasons over claims and evidence, and \citet{lorefact} show that such
pipelines miss errors in the logic connecting claims. Consistency metrics check a text against a source
or across samples, generations, dialogues and beliefs
\citep{factcc,summac,alignscore,minicheck,selfcheckgpt,mundler2024,welleck2019,decode,ribeiro2019,li2019logic,elazar2021,becel},
and reasoning-chain metrics score explicit derivations \citep{roscoe,receval,entailmentbank,lightman2024}.
None says how supports and conflicts combine into one score under a committed world in which refuting
a falsehood counts as support.

\paragraph{Discourse coherence} rests on cohesive ties, entity continuity or inferred relations
\citep{hallidayhasan1976,grosz1995,hobbs1979,mann,pdtb} and is scored by entity grids, neural models,
DiscoScore, entity graphs and LLM judges \citep{barzilay,li,discoscore,liustrube,geval,zheng2023judge}
against human ratings \citep{gcdc}, without regard to the truth of the claims.

\paragraph{Argumentation, constraints and confirmation.} Argument mining recovers support and attack
relations \citep{stab2017}, quality is rated for cogency \citep{wachsmuth2017,habernal2016,persing2015},
and gradual semantics for bipolar argumentation grade arguments under axioms such as neutrality and
monotony \citep{cayrol2005,amgoud2016,amgoud2018,baroni2019}, our closest precedent, though they grade
acceptability rather than accordance with a committed truth assignment. Coherence as constraint
satisfaction \citep{thagard1989,thagard1998} scores a partition into accepted and rejected elements by
the weight of the constraints it satisfies; read at the verdicts this is close to our signed sum, which
we derive, with its asymmetry, as the first order of a probabilistic model. Probabilistic
argumentation constrains beliefs \citep{thimm2012,hunter2013}, and BeliefBank, ConCoRD and Maieutic
prompting repair them with weighted MAX-SAT or factor graphs \citep{beliefbank,concord,maieutic,berant2011},
as in Markov logic and PSL \citep{mln,psl}; we use such a model to \emph{measure} a response. Bayesian
coherence measures \citep{shogenji1999,olsson2002,fitelson2003,douven2007,bovens2003} grade a set of
propositions rather than relations against truth values; $\LCS$ is the logistic of the weight of
evidence \citep{good1950,milne1996,fitelson1999,crupi2007} for the committed world
(App.~\ref{app:verify}).
