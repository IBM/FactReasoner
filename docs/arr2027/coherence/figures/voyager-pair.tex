  density.\,\claimref{2} \emph{That said, the probe cannot really be counted as

  \textbf{in fact} failed permanently before the crossing,\,\claimref{4}

\smallskip
{\footnotesize\raggedright
\textcolor{orange!55!black}{\textbf{The diagnostic.}} By the edit identity every support adds and
every conflict subtracts, whatever the context; only the magnitudes depend on the rest of the
model. A's two refutations earn little because the factuality stage already doubts $a_4$ and
$a_5$. B's case for its conclusion runs through the false $a_4$, which is satisfied at $\xs$ and so
earns almost nothing; what remains is its denial of the true $a_3$, the one relation that costs
it.\par}
