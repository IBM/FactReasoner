LoCoBench is synthetic and no corpus rates logical coherence, so we test four properties of $\LCS$
on natural text labelled for related properties (Table~\ref{tab:external}). Claims are sentences
(atomized claims for the essays), relations come from the GPT-6 graph-level miner, and, since
these datasets give no per-claim verdicts, $\LCS$ is read at the asserted world, where every
inference is a support and every contradiction a conflict; brackets give 95\% bootstrap intervals
(App.~\ref{app:external}). Where ChatProtect records which sentence is false, a found
contradiction refutes it, and $\LCS$ rises on all \extCpVwFoundNGpt{} such pairs at the real
verdicts, against falls on all \extCpVwFoundAssertNGpt{} at the asserted world.

\paragraph{Related to, but not a proxy for, discourse coherence.} $\LCS$ reads only the relations
that constrain truth values (App.~\ref{sec:notions}). On SummEval \citep{summeval} it correlates
with expert coherence ratings at $\extSeRhoCohGpt$ \asapCI{\extSeRhoCohGptLo}{\extSeRhoCohGptHi},
against $\extSeJudgeRhoCoh$ for G-Eval, but the test is weak: \extSeEmptyGpt\% of these
three-sentence summaries have no relation. On the rest (exploratory), without restatements $\LCS$
correlates at $\extSeNoRestateNonEmptyRhoCohGpt$
\asapCI{\extSeNoRestateNonEmptyRhoCohGptLo}{\extSeNoRestateNonEmptyRhoCohGptHi}, while the number
of restatements correlates at $\extSeCountRestateRhoCohGpt$: a restatement is consistent, so $\LCS$
counts it as support, but raters penalize the redundancy. $\LCS$ thus rewards repetition.

\paragraph{Detected conflicts lower the score.} With every claim true, adding a contradiction
lowers $\Lambda$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:edit}), so where the miner finds one the fall checks the lemma:
$\LCS$ falls on \extCdAssertEdgeDecGpt{} of \extCdAssertEdgeNGpt{} ContraDoc pairs
\citep{contradoc} and on all \extCpFoundFallGpt{} ChatProtect pairs \citep{mundler2024} (median
change $\extCdEffFoundMedianGpt$ and $\extCpEffFoundMedianGpt$). Over all pairs it falls far more
often than it rises (sign tests, $p<\extCdAssertPGpt$ and $p<\extCpAssertPGpt$), and adding a
consistent sentence raises it (\extCpCtlIncGpt{} rises, \extCpCtlDecGpt{} falls). Missed
contradictions barely move it, so recall bounds the result: the miner finds
\extCdRecallInsertGpt\% of ContraDoc's and \extCpConfStrongGpt\% of ChatProtect's contradictions.
The signed sum falls less often than $\LCS$. The judge falls too, so detection alone does not
separate $\LCS$ from a judge.

\paragraph{Order: within the noise on short responses, not on long documents.} Permuting
self-contained paragraphs changes neither claims, relations nor committed world, so gold-graph
$\LCS$ is unchanged. On LoCoBench, mined $\LCS$ stays within $\tau=\sThreeTol$ on
\extLocoPermInvGpt\% of permutations against \extLocoNoiseInvGpt\% for re-mining the unchanged text.
On ContraDoc's long documents, mined twice per text in one run, permutation moves the score more
than re-mining ($\extMtPermMeanGpt$ against $\extMtNoiseMeanGpt$; 90\% interval of the difference
\extMtEqLoGpt{} to \extMtEqHiGpt): the miner extracts different relations from a reordered long
text (App.~\ref{app:ext-order}). The judge falls on \extLocoPermDecJudge\% and \extCdPermDecJudge\%
of the permutations, as if reordering were incoherence.

\paragraph{Essay quality beyond length.} As a convergent-validity probe, we score \asapEssays{}
ASAP~2.0 essays \citep{crossley2025asap}, rated 1--6 by trained raters, with the full pipeline.
Length correlates with the score at \asapRhoWords. $\LCS$ keeps a partial correlation of
\asapParLcsAsserted{} \asapCI{\asapParLcsAssertedLo}{\asapParLcsAssertedHi} once length is removed
(\asapRobCubic{} to \asapRobBinsSix{} with nonlinear controls), against \asapParJudgeGevalGptSixSol{}
for G-Eval and \asapParWsumAsserted{} for the signed sum. A rubric-prompted grader tracks the score
better (\asapParJudgeRubricGptSixSol), but $\LCS$ keeps \asapPwrLcsAsserted{}
\asapCI{\asapPwrLcsAssertedLo}{\asapPwrLcsAssertedHi} once both length and the grader are removed,
and argumentative connectives carry nothing beyond length (\asapParConnectives). One prompt and
one miner make this indicative (App.~\ref{app:asap}).
