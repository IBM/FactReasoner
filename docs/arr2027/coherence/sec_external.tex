\section{External Evaluation}
\label{sec:exp-external}
%% ============================================================================

LoCoBench is synthetic, so we also tested the score on natural text that others built and
labelled. Five datasets have analyses, tests and thresholds
pre-registered before any of their graphs was mined: ContraDoc \citep{contradoc} (documents with an
inserted self-contradiction), SummEval \citep{summeval} (summaries with expert coherence ratings),
ChatProtect \citep{mundler2024} (LLM self-contradictions), QT30 \citep{qt30} (debates annotated with
inference and conflict) and GDTB \citep{gdtb} (PDTB-style discourse relations). Paragraph-shuffled
LoCoBench responses complete the order check. A sixth set, not pre-registered, has \asapEssays{} persuasive essays from ASAP~2.0 \citep{crossley2025asap},
scored 1--6 by trained raters. Claims are sentences (QT30: propositions; GDTB: relation arguments;
essays: atomized). Relations come from the
graph-level miner with GPT-6 and with gpt-oss-120b, and $\LCS$ uses flat priors and, unless stated,
the asserted world. Table~\ref{tab:external} lists the findings that hold, with GPT-6;
App.~\ref{app:external} gives the data, the protocol, two logged deviations and every result.

\paragraph{Logical, not discourse, coherence.} $\LCS$ is uncorrelated with SummEval's expert
coherence ratings, which G-Eval tracks. Reordering does not move the GPT-6 score beyond its
re-mining noise (Figure~\ref{fig:external}a), while the judge falls on most permutations, as if
reordering were incoherence. For the noisier gpt-oss-120b, order matters somewhat beyond the noise.

\paragraph{Contradictions and signs.} Where the miner finds the inserted contradiction (exploratory), $\LCS$ falls
on \extCdAssertEdgeDecGpt{} of \extCdAssertEdgeNGpt{} ContraDoc pairs and on all
\extCpFoundFallGpt{} ChatProtect pairs, so on ChatProtect every rise is a missed contradiction. On
ContraDoc's clean documents, \extCdFpGpt\% of graphs contain a contradiction, the false-alarm rate.
Signs follow the truth values in every case tested: at the real verdicts, the ChatProtect
contradictions found are \extCpRealSupportGpt{} supports (a sentence of the pair is false) and
\extCpRealConflictGpt{} conflicts, all of which a contradiction count would charge.

\paragraph{Essay quality.} The essays test the full pipeline, outside the guarantee, since every
essay has its own claims. $\LCS$ rises with the raters' score and keeps $\asapParLcsAsserted$ of partial correlation once length is
removed, about as much as G-Eval ($\asapParJudgeGevalGptSixSol$) (Figure~\ref{fig:external}c).
The signal is the density of supported reasoning: the relation count carries most of it
($\asapParRelations$ after length), and the miner finds only \asapConfAsserted{} conflicts in
\asapConfEssaysAsserted{} of the essays.

\paragraph{What does not hold.} Four pre-registered predictions failed with both arms. \textbf{H1a
and H1c} predicted that $\LCS$ stays within $\tau$ under permutation more often than every
baseline; those baselines are deterministic, while the miner's sampling sets the score's floor.
\textbf{H2a$'$} predicted a rise when the inserted sentence is false and its contradiction is
found; but the two versions of a document are mined separately.
\textbf{H2b} predicted higher recall within a sentence or between nearby sentences than across the
document; the order is reversed (Figure~\ref{fig:external}b), partly because a contradiction inside one
sentence cannot be represented by a relation between two claims (\extCdRecallIntraGpt\% found).
\textbf{H3c} also fails with gpt-oss-120b, which finds only \extCpConfStrongOss\% of ChatProtect's
contradictions. On essays, counting claims absent from the source as false lowers the partial
correlation to $\asapParLcsStrict$. Throughout, the binding constraint is relation recall. GPT-6 finds the contradiction in
\extCdRecallGpt\% of ContraDoc's contradicted documents (gpt-oss-120b: \extCdRecallOss\%), and on
QT30 it recovers \extQtRecEntailmentGpt\% of the annotated inferences but only
\extQtRecContradictionGpt\% of the conflicts, partly because its annotators reconstruct implicit
inferences across turns, which our miners are told not to emit.
