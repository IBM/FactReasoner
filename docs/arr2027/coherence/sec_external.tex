LoCoBench is synthetic, and no corpus rates the logical coherence of a response, so we test four
properties of $\LCS$ on natural text labelled for related properties (Table~\ref{tab:external}).
Claims are sentences (atomized claims for the essays), relations come from the GPT-6 graph-level
miner, and, since these datasets give no per-claim verdicts, $\LCS$ is read at the asserted world,
where every inference is a support and every contradiction a conflict. These tests therefore
exercise how the score combines mined relations, not the verdict polarity. Brackets give 95\%
bootstrap intervals; App.~\ref{app:external} has the rest, including an inconclusive
verdict-world test.

\paragraph{Related to, but not a proxy for, discourse coherence.} $\LCS$ reads only the relations
that constrain truth values (App.~\ref{sec:notions}). On SummEval \citep{summeval} it correlates
with expert coherence ratings at $\extSeRhoCohGpt$ \asapCI{\extSeRhoCohGptLo}{\extSeRhoCohGptHi},
against $\extSeJudgeRhoCoh$ for G-Eval, but the test is weak: \extSeEmptyGpt\% of these
three-sentence summaries have no relation and score $\frac12$. On the rest, the two kinds of
support pull apart (exploratory): without restatements $\LCS$ correlates at
$\extSeNoRestateNonEmptyRhoCohGpt$ \asapCI{\extSeNoRestateNonEmptyRhoCohGptLo}{\extSeNoRestateNonEmptyRhoCohGptHi},
as related notions should, while the number of restatements correlates at
$\extSeCountRestateRhoCohGpt$: restating a claim is consistent, so $\LCS$ counts it as support,
but raters penalize the redundancy. $\LCS$ thus rewards repetition, a limitation.

\paragraph{Detected conflicts lower the score.} With every claim true, adding a contradiction
lowers $\Lambda$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:edit}), so where the miner finds one the fall checks the lemma:
$\LCS$ falls on \extCdAssertEdgeDecGpt{} of \extCdAssertEdgeNGpt{} ContraDoc pairs
\citep{contradoc} and on all \extCpFoundFallGpt{} ChatProtect pairs \citep{mundler2024} (median
change $\extCdEffFoundMedianGpt$ and $\extCpEffFoundMedianGpt$). Over all pairs it falls far more
often than it rises (sign tests, $p<\extCdAssertPGpt$ and $p<\extCpAssertPGpt$), while adding a
sentence that does not contradict the first raises it (\extCpCtlIncGpt{} rises, \extCpCtlDecGpt{}
falls). Missed contradictions barely move it (median $\extCdEffNotFoundMedianGpt$), so recall bounds
the result: the miner finds \extCdRecallInsertGpt\% of ContraDoc's inserted contradictions and
\extCpConfStrongGpt\% of ChatProtect's, with false alarms in \extCdFpGpt\% of clean documents. The
signed sum falls less often than $\LCS$ (Table~\ref{tab:external}). The judge falls too, so
detecting contradictions alone does not separate $\LCS$ from a judge; order and LoCoBench do.

\paragraph{Order moves the score about as much as re-mining.} Permuting self-contained paragraphs
leaves the claims, relations and committed world unchanged, so gold-graph $\LCS$ is identical under
permutation; a mined score also carries sampling noise. On LoCoBench, $\LCS$ stays within
$\tau=\sThreeTol$ on \extLocoPermInvGpt\% of permutations against \extLocoNoiseInvGpt\% for
re-mining the unchanged text, a difference in mean change of $\extLocoEqDiffGpt$ (90\% interval
up to \extLocoEqHiGpt), so equivalence within $\tau$ is narrowly not established. On ContraDoc's
long documents re-mining itself keeps only \extCdNoiseInvGpt\% and drifts upwards, so the test has
little power (App.~\ref{app:ext-order}). The judge falls on \extLocoPermDecJudge\% and
\extCdPermDecJudge\% of the permutations, as if reordering were incoherence.

\paragraph{Essay quality beyond length.} As a convergent-validity probe, we score \asapEssays{}
persuasive essays from ASAP~2.0 \citep{crossley2025asap}, rated 1--6 by trained raters, with the full
pipeline from claim extraction to mining. Length correlates with the score at \asapRhoWords.
$\LCS$ keeps a partial correlation of \asapParLcsAsserted{}
\asapCI{\asapParLcsAssertedLo}{\asapParLcsAssertedHi} once length is removed (\asapRobCubic{} to
\asapRobBinsSix{} with nonlinear controls), against \asapParJudgeGevalGptSixSol{} for G-Eval and
\asapParWsumAsserted{} for the signed sum. Argumentative connectives carry nothing beyond length
(\asapParConnectives), and $\LCS$ keeps \asapRobWordsConnectives{} when they are removed too. One prompt
and one miner make the result indicative (App.~\ref{app:asap}).
