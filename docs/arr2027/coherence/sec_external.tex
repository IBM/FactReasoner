LoCoBench is synthetic and no corpus rates logical coherence, so we test four properties of $\LCS$
on natural text labeled for related properties (Table~\ref{tab:external}). Claims are sentences
(atomized claims for the essays) and relations come from the GPT-6 graph-level miner. Except where
stated, $\LCS$ is read at the asserted world, where every inference is a support and every
contradiction a conflict, so these tests exercise how the score combines mined relations; brackets
give 95\% bootstrap intervals, and the tests fixed before mining are in Table~\ref{tab:ext-plan}. Every positive result below is at the asserted world, where $\LCS$ behaves much like a count of
mined relations; at the verdict world, ContraDoc's test fixed in advance failed
($p=\extCdVerdEdgePGpt$; App.~\ref{app:ext-further}). The simplifications behave like $\LCS$ on most
rows (Table~\ref{tab:external}); the exceptions are noted below.

\paragraph{Not a proxy for discourse coherence.} $\LCS$ reads only relations that constrain truth values (App.~\ref{sec:notions}); this discriminant
check, which a constant would pass, shows only what the score is not. On SummEval \citep{summeval} it correlates with expert
coherence ratings at $\extSeRhoCohGpt$ \asapCI{\extSeRhoCohGptLo}{\extSeRhoCohGptHi}, against
$\extSeJudgeRhoCoh$ for G-Eval; \extSeEmptyGpt\% of these three-sentence summaries have no relation,
and on the rest the correlation is negative, $\extSeNonEmptyRhoCohGpt$
\asapCI{\extSeNonEmptyRhoCohGptLo}{\extSeNonEmptyRhoCohGptHi}. Two opposite effects meet there (exploratory). Summaries with more mined restatements are rated less
coherent ($\extSeCountRestateRhoCohGpt$), and a restatement is an equivalence, which the first-order
weights credit twice an inference ($v_r=\frac12$ against $\frac14$): $\Llin$ shows the same
$\extSeLinNonEmptyRhoCohGpt$, the signed sum, which credits both alike, $\extSeWsumNonEmptyRhoCohGpt$. On
summaries with inferences only, $\LCS$ correlates positively, $\extSeInfOnlyRhoCohGpt$
\asapCI{\extSeInfOnlyRhoCohGptLo}{\extSeInfOnlyRhoCohGptHi}. It holds within each of the 16 summarizers ($\LCS$ $\extSeWithinLcsGpt$, restatements
$\extSeWithinRestateGpt$): $\LCS$ rewards restating a claim.

\paragraph{Detected conflicts lower the score.} With every claim true, adding a contradiction lowers $\Lambda$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:edit}); the two versions
are mined separately, so the fall is predicted rather than guaranteed:
$\LCS$ falls on \extCdAssertEdgeDecGpt{} of \extCdAssertEdgeNGpt{} ContraDoc pairs
\citep{contradoc} and on all \extCpFoundFallGpt{} ChatProtect pairs \citep{mundler2024} (median
change $\extCdEffFoundMedianGpt$ and $\extCpEffFoundMedianGpt$). Over all pairs it falls far more often than it rises, also by document and topic (App.~\ref{app:ext-contra}), and a consistent sentence raises it (\extCpCtlIncGpt{} rises, \extCpCtlDecGpt{} falls). Missed
contradictions barely move it (on ContraDoc \extCdAssertNotFoundDecGpt{} falls against
\extCdAssertNotFoundIncGpt{} rises), so recall bounds the result: the miner finds
\extCdRecallInsertGpt\% of ContraDoc's inserted contradictions (\extCdRecallGpt\% over all edits) and
\extCpConfStrongGpt\% of ChatProtect's, and \extCdFpGpt\% of clean ContraDoc graphs contain a
contradiction. No paired difference with the signed sum that could reflect the contradictions survives Holm; on
ChatProtect $\LCS$ falls alone more often (\extCpMcAllOnlyLcsGpt{} against \extCpMcAllOnlySumGpt) because
the added claim changes the prior of the committed world (App.~\ref{app:ext-contra}). G-Eval falls on
\extCdJudgeDec{} ContraDoc pairs and never rises.

\paragraph{Order.} Mined twice per text in one run, $\LCS$ moves slightly more under paragraph
permutation than under re-mining (90\% intervals of the difference in mean change: LoCoBench
\extLmEqLoGpt{} to \extLmEqHiGpt, ContraDoc by section \extSecEqLoGpt{} to \extSecEqHiGpt), with
test-retest ICC \extLmIccLcsAllGpt{} and \extSecIccLcsAllGpt{} (judge \extLmIccJudgeAll{} and
\extSecIccJudgeAll; App.~\ref{app:ext-order}). G-Eval falls on \extLmJudgePermDec\% and \extSecJudgePermDec\% of permutations (\extLmJudgeNoiseDec\%
and \extSecJudgeNoiseDec\% re-judged), as a rater of discourse coherence may; the order tests fixed
in advance failed for $\LCS$ (App.~\ref{app:ext-order}).

\paragraph{Essay quality beyond length.} We score \asapEssays{} ASAP~2.0 essays
\citep{crossley2025asap}, rated 1--6 by trained raters, from atomized claims and mined relations at the asserted world;
sampling 20 essays per score level inflates every correlation, and the analysis is exploratory.
Length correlates with the score at \asapRhoWords. $\LCS$ keeps a partial correlation of
\asapParLcsAsserted{} \asapCI{\asapParLcsAssertedLo}{\asapParLcsAssertedHi} once length is removed
(\asapRobCubic{} to \asapRobBinsSix{} with nonlinear controls), the relation count
\asapParRelations, the signed sum \asapParWsumAsserted{} and G-Eval \asapParJudgeGevalGptSixSol, with no
difference resolved: the essay signal is carried by mined relations in general, not by how
$\LCS$ combines them. A rubric-prompted 0--100 grader correlates more (\asapParJudgeRubricOneZeroZeroGptSixSol), though its
difference from $\LCS$ is not resolved; $\LCS$ keeps
\asapPwrhLcsAsserted{} after length and the grader, not resolvably more than the relation count
(App.~\ref{app:asap}).
