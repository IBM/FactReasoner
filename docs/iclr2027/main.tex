\begin{abstract}
Long-form factuality assessment decomposes a response into atomic claims and verifies each
against retrieved evidence, treating the claims as independent. It therefore cannot see a
response whose atoms are individually true but which does not hang together as an argument,
because a later sentence retracts an earlier one or because the inferential links it draws do
not hold. We formalize \emph{logical coherence} as a property of the joint distribution over
atom truth values induced by a Markov random field whose pairwise factors are the relations
the response itself draws. The relation vocabulary is a two-level taxonomy: twelve
interpretable discourse senses, grounded in PDTB~3.0 and RST, compiling deterministically onto
five inferential couplings, each of which fixes a factor table by naming the world it
penalizes. We define four coherence readouts, give the auxiliary-variable construction each
requires, and argue for the mean posterior marginal on five stated criteria. Atom priors come
from an existing factuality pipeline, making coherence and factuality two stages over one
decomposition rather than competing scores. Validating such a measure needs data that varies
coherence while holding content fixed, which no benchmark provides. We therefore introduce
\textbf{LoCoBench}, a generator emitting \emph{coherence ladders} --- families of five
responses over one claim set whose relative ordering is declared in advance --- with an
evaluation protocol scoring a measure on whether it recovers those orderings. Gold relation
graphs satisfy $78.6\%$ of the declared constraints; graphs mined from the response text reach
$56.3\%$ coupling-level F1 and $53.6\%$ of the constraints, locating the bottleneck in
relation extraction rather than the probabilistic model. We release the taxonomy, the
generator, and the harness.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec-intro}
%% ================================================================

The dominant paradigm for assessing the factuality of long-form generation is
decompose-then-verify: split a response into atomic claims, check each against retrieved
evidence, and aggregate the verdicts \citep{factscore,safe,veriscore}. The paradigm is
effective and now standard. It also embeds an assumption --- that the truth of each atom
can be settled in isolation --- which discards exactly the structure that makes prose an
\emph{argument} rather than a list.

Two failure modes escape it. A response can assert atoms that are individually supported but
mutually inconsistent: a report stating both that no one was harmed and that three passengers
died contains a true claim and a false one, and per-atom verification says so, but it does not
register that the response contradicts \emph{itself}. And the inferential links a response
draws may not hold even when their endpoints do; a chain of true statements joined by a
spurious ``therefore'' is not a sound argument. \citet{lorefact} quantify the gap: on
logic-related samples, SAFE, VeriScore and FactCheck-GPT omit the logical dimension on
$60\%$, $78\%$ and $48\%$ of cases respectively.

\textbf{Factuality and coherence are orthogonal axes.} A response can be a bag of true facts
that is incoherent, and it can be internally impeccable but wrong. Our running example
(\S\ref{sec-examples}) is a recall report carrying an unresolved conflict; a second fixture
pair summarizes one appellate judgment twice from the \emph{same} facts, differing only in the
order the claims are asserted, and only one is coherent. No amount of per-atom grounding
separates them.

This paper measures the second axis, taking coherence to be a statement about a \emph{joint}
distribution: given what a response asserts and how it links its assertions, how much of it
does the argument, taken as a whole, uphold? That question is answered by a marginal in a
graphical model whose variables are atom truth values and whose factors are the asserted
relations --- not by a count of violated rules.

Our contributions are: (i) a \textbf{coherence MRF with a formal two-level relation taxonomy}
(\S\ref{sec-coherence}) --- twelve discourse senses compiling deterministically onto five
inferential couplings, each defined by the assignment it penalizes, which keeps the annotation
vocabulary rich while the model stays a closed three-parameter pairwise family; (ii) \textbf{four
readouts with a selection argument} (\S\ref{sec-readouts}), each with its auxiliary-variable
construction, showing the mean posterior marginal to be the only one monotone in conflict
resolution, constant-free and bounded, and why two natural alternatives fail --- one by scoring a
disconnected bag of atoms perfectly; (iii) \textbf{two-stage composition} with factuality
(\S\ref{sec-twostage}); and (iv) \textbf{LoCoBench} (\S\ref{sec-locobench}), a generator of
coherence ladders with committee validation, an evaluation protocol and baselines
(\S\ref{sec-baselines}), and \textbf{measurements} (\S\ref{sec-experiments}) separating
extraction error from scoring error by swapping only the relation graph.

\section{Related work}
\label{sec-related}
%% ================================================================

\textbf{Atomic factuality.} FActScore \citep{factscore} introduced per-atom precision against
a knowledge source; SAFE \citep{safe} added search-augmented verification and $F_1@K$;
VeriScore \citep{veriscore} restricted attention to verifiable claims. FactReasoner
\citep{factreasoner} replaced verdict counting with probabilistic inference over a factor graph
of atom and context variables with NLI-derived pairwise factors. We reuse its decomposition,
relation extractor and inference stack, and take its marginals as priors
(\S\ref{sec-twostage}); coherence is a different question asked of the same decomposition.

\textbf{The logic gap.} \citet{lorefact} is the closest prior art and states the problem:
decompose-then-verify pipelines verify atoms individually and overlook the logical dependencies
among them, so a text combining true facts through a false dependency is misjudged as factual.
Our departure is to model those dependencies as factors in a joint distribution and read
coherence off its marginals, rather than treating them as a labelling task.

\textbf{Discourse coherence.} Entity-grid models \citep{barzilay} and neural successors
\citep{li} score coherence from surface transition statistics, trained against synthetic
perturbations --- shuffling and contradiction injection. LoCoBench extends that methodology by
making the perturbation an \emph{operator on a declared relation graph} rather than on raw
text, so its intended effect on the score is known per rung. DiscoScore \citep{discoscore} uses
BERT-based discourse representations. Our senses come from PDTB~3.0 \citep{pdtb} and RST
\citep{mann}, but as an interpretable layer compiling to a smaller inferential vocabulary,
which is what makes them usable as factor types.

\textbf{Consistency checking, judges, and relational models.} SelfCheckGPT
\citep{selfcheckgpt} detects hallucination via pairwise NLI over sampled responses; its NLI
variant is the natural ablation isolating what global inference adds over local detection.
G-Eval \citep{geval} is the practical incumbent --- an LLM judge scoring coherence directly.
Markov logic \citep{mln} is the natural alternative encoding; Appendix~\ref{app-mln} shows a
naive one-clause-per-relation encoding is \emph{not} equivalent to our MRF and is degenerate at
its mode, while a three-clause expansion reproduces it exactly on the three-coupling
fragment.

\section{Stage 1: atom probabilities}
\label{sec-factuality}
%% ================================================================

\input{figures/pipeline}

We take atom-level factuality probabilities from FactReasoner \citep{factreasoner}; we summarize
it because the coherence model consumes its output and reuses its machinery
(Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline}). A response $y$ is decomposed into atomic claims
$\mathcal{A}=\{a_1,\dots,a_n\}$, each decontextualized so that it is independently checkable, and
a retriever returns contexts $\mathcal{C}=\{c_1,\dots,c_m\}$. An LLM-based NLI extractor labels
each admitted pair with $r\in\{\text{entailment},\text{contradiction},\text{neutral}\}$ and a
confidence $p^\ast$; context--context pairs are scored both ways and entailment in both
directions is reconciled to \emph{equivalence}. Neutral verdicts are dropped and contribute no
factor, leaving the atom at its prior. The confidence is read off the generation itself,

\begin{equation}
p^\ast \;=\; \exp\!\Big(\tfrac{1}{|T|}\textstyle\sum_{t\in T}\log p_t\Big),
\qquad T=\{\text{tokens overlapping the emitted label span}\},
\label{eq-pstar}
\end{equation}

the per-token geometric-mean probability of the label the model actually produced. Measuring
exactly the span the label parser reports makes label and probability structurally unable to
disagree, which matters because bracket delimiters are frequently fused into subword tokens.
Where a backend exposes no logprobs, $p^\ast$ comes from SIMBA-UQ \citep{simbauq} instead.

The graph becomes a pairwise MRF --- one binary variable per atom and per context, unary factors
$\phi_X(x)=[1-\pi_X,\pi_X]$, one pairwise factor per relation from Table~\ref{tab:factors} ---
and marginals are computed by weighted mini-bucket elimination \citep{wmb} with i-bound $6$. Of
its three graph variants we use \textsc{fr2}, which deduplicates contexts and scores every atom
against all of them. We report the with-priors tables with $\pi_C=0.9$, $\pi_A=0.5$; the
published model uses the no-priors tables and $\pi_C=0.99$. The difference is immaterial to the
coherence results, which set atom priors explicitly, but the tables are not interchangeable
(\S\ref{sec-potentials}).

\section{Stage 2: the coherence MRF}
\label{sec-coherence}
%% ================================================================

\subsection{The model}

\begin{definition}[Coherence MRF]
\label{def-mrf}
Let $\mathcal{A}=\langle a_1,\dots,a_n\rangle$ be the atoms of a response in assertion
order, and let each $a_i$ be a binary variable with $a_i=1$ read as ``the claim holds''.
Let $\mathcal{R}=\{(s_r,t_r,\tau_r,p_r)\}_{r=1}^{|\mathcal{R}|}$ be a set of
\emph{relations}, where $s_r\neq t_r$ index atoms, $\tau_r$ is an inferential coupling
(Def.~\ref{def-couplings}) and $p_r\in[0,1]$ is the relation's weight. The coherence MRF
induced by $(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{R})$ and priors $\pi\in[0,1]^n$ is
\begin{equation}
\pr{\mathbf{a}} \;=\; \frac{1}{Z}\;
  \prod_{i=1}^{n}\phi_i(a_i)\;
  \prod_{r\in\mathcal{R}}\psi_{\tau_r,p_r}\!\left(a_{s_r},a_{t_r}\right),
\qquad
Z=\sum_{\mathbf{a}\in\{0,1\}^n}\prod_i\phi_i\prod_r\psi_r ,
\label{eq-joint}
\end{equation}
with $\phi_i(a_i)=[1-\pi_i,\;\pi_i]$ and $\psi_{\tau,p}$ given by
Table~\ref{tab:factors}.
\end{definition}

Two properties matter. The factor family is \emph{closed}: a coupling is only a choice of which
cells of a $2\times2$ table are down-weighted, so no relation type can smuggle in extra
expressiveness. And the model is order-sensitive \emph{only} through the relations --- atom order
enters via which pairs are related and in which direction, never a positional term --- so an
order-invariant edit provably cannot move the score, which the \textsc{control} ladder of
\S\ref{sec-locobench} tests empirically.

\subsection{Level 1: inferential couplings}

\begin{definition}[Inferential couplings]
\label{def-couplings}
A coupling $\tau$ is specified by the set of joint assignments $(a_s,a_t)$ it penalizes.
The vocabulary is
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llcl@{}}
\toprule
Coupling & Reading & Directed & Penalized world(s) \\
\midrule
\coupling{entailment}   & $a_s \Rightarrow a_t$              & yes & $(1,0)$ \\
\coupling{contradiction}& $a_s \Rightarrow \neg a_t$         & yes & $(1,1)$ \\
\coupling{equivalence}  & $a_s \Leftrightarrow a_t$          & no  & $(0,1)$ and $(1,0)$ \\
\coupling{exclusive}    & exactly one of $a_s,a_t$ holds     & no  & $(0,0)$ and $(1,1)$ \\
\coupling{co\_necessity}& at least one of $a_s,a_t$ holds    & no  & $(0,0)$ \\
\coupling{none}         & no coupling                        & --- & none (no factor) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\end{definition}

The first three are the NLI edge types of \S\ref{sec-factuality}, so a mined relation round-trips
into the existing machinery unchanged; the last two are additions required by argumentative prose.
\coupling{exclusive} is strictly stronger than \coupling{contradiction}, forbidding the both-false
world as well --- what \emph{exhaustive} alternatives assert (``no one was harmed'' versus ``three
people died'' can neither both hold nor both fail) --- and \coupling{co\_necessity} is its dual. We
call $\{\coupling{contradiction},\coupling{exclusive}\}$ the \emph{conflict} couplings, being
those that down-weight the both-true world.

The with-priors tables are \emph{row-stochastic} --- each source-state row sums to one, giving
$\pr{a_t{=}1\mid a_s{=}0}=\pi_s$ and $\pr{a_t{=}1\mid a_s{=}1}=p$ for \coupling{entailment} ---
and this is the section's substantive modelling choice. Under the no-priors table the $(1,0)$ cell
charges the \emph{source} being true whenever the target is uncertain: appropriate when the source
is a retrieved context whose own truth is at stake, wrong for coherence, where a well-supported
premise must not be penalized for being a premise. The consequence is not cosmetic: on the example
of \S\ref{sec-examples} the spine's root collapses to $\pr{a_1{=}1}=0.19$ and the mean marginal
stays at $0.46$ \emph{even after every conflict is removed}.

\subsection{Level 2: discourse senses and the compile map}

Couplings are the right vocabulary for a factor table and the wrong one for annotation: asked
whether two claims stand in \coupling{entailment}, an annotator has been given a question about
logic when the observable fact is one about discourse. We therefore mine an interpretable layer
and compile it down.

\begin{definition}[Compile map]
\label{def-compile}
Let $\mathcal{S}$ be the set of Level-2 discourse senses. The compile map
$\mathcal{C}:\mathcal{S}\to\mathcal{T}\times([0,1]\cup\{\bot\})\times\{0,1\}^3$ assigns to
each sense a coupling, a strength prior ($\bot$ = use the estimator's own value), and the
flags \emph{directed}, \emph{concession} and \emph{ordering-only}. It is given in
Table~\ref{tab:compile} and is total and deterministic.
\end{definition}

\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The \sense{Contrast}/\sense{Alternative} boundary is the distinction the taxonomy exists to draw,
and it is exactly the exhaustiveness question: claims that merely cannot both hold are a
\coupling{contradiction}, those that additionally cannot both fail are \coupling{exclusive}. A
measure conflating them mis-scores every adjudicated dispute, since the point of a finding is to
close an exhaustive pair.

\paragraph{Concession discount.} A \sense{Concession} is a conflict the text itself
resolves --- ``the airline argued pilot error, but the report found a metallurgical
defect'' is not an incoherence if the report's finding is asserted as authoritative. When a
resolving \emph{holding} atom $h$ is present, the conflict's weight is discounted,
\begin{equation}
p_r^{\text{eff}} \;=\; p_r\,\bigl(1-\lambda\,\pi_h\bigr),
\label{eq-concession}
\end{equation}
with $\lambda=0.45$; absent a resolver the relation stands at full strength. Holdings are
identified by cue phrases (\emph{held}, \emph{ruled}, \emph{concluded}, \emph{the tribunal})
on an atom that is not itself an endpoint.

\subsection{Estimating relations from the response}
\label{sec-mining}

The relation weight decomposes into a type term and a strength term,
\begin{equation}
p_r \;=\; \underbrace{\pr{\tau \mid a_s,a_t,y}}_{\text{type confidence}}\;\cdot\;
          \underbrace{\pr{a_t \mid a_s,\tau,y}}_{\text{conditional strength}},
\label{eq-decomp}
\end{equation}
estimated by two prompts. \textbf{Prompt A} elicits the discourse sense and coupling, with
chain-of-thought \emph{before} the label so the type confidence reads off the label span's token
logprobs exactly as in Eq.~\ref{eq-pstar}. \textbf{Prompt B} elicits the conditional strength as a
surrogate Yes/No token, renormalized as $\pr{\text{Yes}}/(\pr{\text{Yes}}+\pr{\text{No}})$
\citep{kadavath}; a verbalized alternative is kept only for comparison, verbalized confidence
being weakly calibrated \citep{xiong}.

\paragraph{Grounding is mandatory.} Prompt A always sees the response and asserts a coupling only
if the response \emph{itself} draws the connection. Judging a pair in isolation makes a model
accept any abstractly plausible relation, over-connecting the graph and --- the score being a
product of many soft constraints --- collapsing the marginals toward the prior even for a coherent
response. Two further precision mechanisms: an \emph{admissibility filter} restricting output to
the nine $(\text{sense},\text{coupling})$ combinations marked $\star$ in
Table~\ref{tab:compile}, and a required \emph{verifiable evidence span} --- a verbatim quotation
from the response, checked by the parser rather than merely requested.

\paragraph{Candidate pairs.} Mining all $n(n-1)$ ordered pairs is quadratic and, worse,
over-connects. We define four policies --- \textsc{all\_pairs}; \textsc{windowed} (forward pairs
with $0<j-i\le w$, $w=4$); \textsc{gated} (the window plus long-range forward pairs passing a
similarity gate); and \textsc{bidirectional} (both arc directions within $|j-i|\le w$) --- and
refine all of them against the response, \emph{promoting} out-of-window pairs the prose links by
shared entity or sentence adjacency and \emph{demoting} in-window pairs it does not link
(Appendix~\ref{app-pairs}). \textsc{bidirectional} exists because a forward-only policy
\emph{cannot emit} a directed arc running backward in atom order, so such gold relations are
unreachable under it regardless of the model --- a structural cap we measure in
\S\ref{sec-experiments}.

\section{Four coherence readouts}
\label{sec-readouts}
%% ================================================================

The MRF defines a distribution; a score is a functional of it. We give four candidates, then argue
for one.

\paragraph{(a) Mean marginal.} The headline readout,
\begin{equation}
\LCS_{\mm}(y) \;=\; \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\pr{a_i=1},
\label{eq-mm}
\end{equation}
read directly off the marginals: the expected fraction of the response's atoms that the argument,
taken as a whole, upholds. A conflict suppresses its endpoints' marginals; a satisfied entailment
chain propagates support along itself.

\paragraph{(b) Consistency.} A two-term readout separating the absence of conflict from
the presence of support:
\begin{equation}
\LCS_{\cons} = \tfrac12\bigl(\mathcal{K}+\mathcal{S}\bigr),\quad
\mathcal{K} = \pr{\bigwedge_{r:\,\tau_r=\con}\neg\bigl(a_{s_r}\!\wedge a_{t_r}\bigr)},\quad
\mathcal{S} = \frac{\sum_{r\in\mathcal{R}_{\mathcal{S}}} p_r\,U_r}
                   {\sum_{r\in\mathcal{R}_{\mathcal{S}}} p_r},
\label{eq-cons}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{R}_{\mathcal{S}}$ ranges over
$\{\coupling{entailment},\coupling{equivalence},\coupling{exclusive}\}$ and $U_r$ is the
probability that $r$ is \emph{actively upheld}: $\pr{a_{s}{=}1\wedge a_{t}{=}1}$ for
entailment and equivalence, $\pr{a_s\neq a_t}$ for exclusive.

The two coupling sets differ deliberately. $\mathcal{K}$ keys on \coupling{contradiction} alone:
an \coupling{exclusive} spreads its incoherence over \emph{both} same-value worlds, so reading
only its both-true half describes half the coupling and reports the other half as fine. It earns
credit in $\mathcal{S}$ instead, where its exactly-one world is both where the exclusion is
honoured and where it is informative. \coupling{co\_necessity} enters neither term: its defect is
the both-false world, which (a)'s marginals already see. $\mathcal{K}$ is computed exactly with
deterministic auxiliary variables --- one $u_r=a_{s_r}\!\wedge a_{t_r}$ per conflict edge, then a
chained disjunction $U=\bigvee_r u_r$, and $\mathcal{K}=\pr{U{=}0}$ --- all at most ternary, so no
$2^{|\mathcal{R}|}$ table is built. $\mathcal{S}$ needs its own pass, being a weighted sum of
\emph{pairwise joints}: a linear functional of the joint, not an event probability.

\begin{proposition}[Why upholding, not satisfaction]
\label{prop-vacuous}
Let $\mathcal{S}'$ credit a relation when it is merely \emph{satisfied} rather than
actively upheld, and let $\mathcal{K}'=\pr{\text{all relations satisfied}}$. Then a response
with no mined relations receives $\mathcal{K}'=1$, the maximum.
\end{proposition}

The proposition is immediate --- an empty product is one --- but its consequence is not. The
obvious repair to $\mathcal{K}$, extending the conflict event to ``all relations honoured'',
makes matters strictly worse: on the family of \S\ref{sec-examples} a disconnected bag of atoms
scores $1.000$ while a response with a fully satisfied causal spine scores $0.221$, converting a
tie into an inversion, and every count-normalized variant we tried (geometric mean, expected
fraction satisfied) is either still $1.000$ on the bag or non-monotone across the ladder. The
root cause is that ``are the mined relations honoured?'' is a property of the \emph{relation
set}, not of the response: it is maximized by asserting nothing. Crediting only the informative
world $(1,1)$ removes the free lunch, since a bag of atoms upholds nothing and scores
$\mathcal{S}=0$. The terms are averaged, not multiplied, so neither can zero out the other.

\paragraph{(c) Reified coherence node.} Add a binary variable $R$ with prior $\rho$ and one
ternary vote factor per relation, charging $1-p_r$ in the $R{=}1$ branch when $r$ is violated at
$(a_s,a_t)$ and flat in the $R{=}0$ branch; then $\LCS_{\rei}=\pr{R=1}$
(Appendix~\ref{app-reified}). \emph{Violated} is the penalized set of Def.~\ref{def-couplings}, so
\coupling{entailment} counts as satisfied even vacuously at $a_s=0$.

\paragraph{(d) Normalized log-partition.} The partition function measures how much probability
mass the constraints leave, graded between two references from the same edge skeleton:
\begin{equation}
\LCS_{\lp} \;=\; \frac{\log Z - \log Z_{\min}}{\log Z_{\max} - \log Z_{\min}} .
\label{eq-logz}
\end{equation}
$Z_{\max}$ removes all conflict edges --- dropping constraint factors only adds mass, so
$\log Z\le\log Z_{\max}$. The floor is the \emph{MAP world mass} of the base network,
$Z_{\min}=\max_{\mathbf{a}}\prod_i\phi_i\prod_r\psi_r$.

\begin{lemma}
\label{lem-floor}
$\log Z_{\min}\le \log Z$ for any network, and $Z_{\min}$ is coherence-graded.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
$Z=\sum_{\mathbf{a}}\text{mass}(\mathbf{a})$ is a sum of non-negative terms, so the single
largest term bounds it below. Strengthening a conflict lowers the mass of every world that
violates it, including the most probable one, so the bound moves with coherence.
\end{proof}

Lemma~\ref{lem-floor} is not a technicality: two natural skeleton-derived floors --- retyping
every edge to \coupling{contradiction}, or saturating conflicts to $p=1$ --- are \emph{not}
valid lower bounds for row-stochastic tables, because an isolated conflict removes no mass while
a mass-concentrating entailment backbone concentrates a great deal. Empirically the base
$\log Z$ fell below both on real graphs, putting Eq.~\ref{eq-logz} outside $[0,1]$.

\subsection{Which readout, and why}

We judge the four on five criteria: \textbf{R1} a functional of the joint, not of the relation
list; \textbf{R2} bounded in $[0,1]$ and interpretable; \textbf{R3} monotone --- resolving a
conflict never lowers the score; \textbf{R4} no hand-tuned constants; \textbf{R5} computable
from standard inference queries. Only (a) and (d) satisfy all five
(Table~\ref{tab:criteria}). Readouts (b) and (c) fail R3 for one shared reason worth stating
precisely, because it looks like a bug and is not.

\begin{proposition}[Belief versus event activity]
\label{prop-quirk}
Softening a resolved conflict via Eq.~\ref{eq-concession} \emph{raises} the probability of
its both-true world. For a lone conflict edge with uniform priors, discounting $p$ from
$0.80$ to $0.55$ moves $\pr{a_s{=}1,a_t{=}1}$ from $0.10$ to $0.225$.
\end{proposition}

The both-true cell is $1-p$ for both conflict couplings, so weakening the constraint makes the
forbidden world more probable. A readout asking \emph{is the conflict active} --- (b) counts that
event, (c) charges $1-p_r$ --- therefore registers a softened conflict as \emph{more} often
active and dips, while one asking \emph{how strongly do we believe each atom} --- (a) via
marginals, (d) via mass --- sees less suppression and rises. Resolving a concession should raise
coherence, so a coherence score must track belief, not event activity. We select (a): it meets
all five criteria, needs one \textsc{mar} query, and is the only readout attributable to
individual atoms, hence diagnosable. We report all four, since (b) and (d) carry complementary
information ($\mathcal{K}$ and $\mathcal{S}$ separately, and the mass reading) and (b)'s dip is
a \emph{predicted} inversion that \S\ref{sec-locobench} turns into a testable constraint.

\paragraph{Inference cost.} All four readouts need seven inference calls (base \textsc{mar} and
\textsc{pr}, two consistency passes, reified \textsc{mar}, ceiling \textsc{pr}, base
\textsc{map}); computed independently they would cost fourteen, each repeating the shared base
pair. Production inference is weighted mini-bucket \citep{wmb} with i-bound $6$; every
\emph{exact} value in \S\ref{sec-examples} comes from $2^n$ enumeration, our regression oracle
for $n\le20$.

\section{Composing coherence with factuality}
\label{sec-twostage}
%% ================================================================

The unary priors of Definition~\ref{def-mrf} are a free slot, and the natural thing to put
in them is what Stage 1 already computed (Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline}):
\begin{equation}
\pi_i \;\leftarrow\; q_i \;=\; \pr{a_i=1 \mid \mathcal{C}} .
\label{eq-priors}
\end{equation}
An atom is then credited when it is both externally supported and internally upheld, while setting
$q_i\equiv\tfrac12$ recovers a pure coherence score consulting no evidence --- so the two axes stay
separable and the composition is an option, not a commitment. Two stages rather than one joint
network is deliberate: inference runs over $n$ atom variables rather than $n(1{+}k)$, the coherence
graph can be rescored under several prior sets without re-mining, and the factuality stage stays
independently cacheable. Priors are applied at scoring time, never by mutating the mined graph.

Matching Stage-1 atoms to Stage-2 atoms is done by object identity first, then normalized text,
and only then by id --- text before id deliberately, because near-duplicate removal leaves the
surviving id set sparse, so matching on id alone can attach a prior to the \emph{wrong} atom
(Appendix~\ref{app-align}).

\section{LoCoBench}
\label{sec-locobench}
%% ================================================================

Validating a coherence measure requires responses that differ in coherence while holding content
fixed. Natural corpora do not supply this: two documents differing in coherence almost always
differ in topic, length and claim set too, so any score difference is unattributable, and human
ratings give a target but not a controlled contrast. What is needed is a family of responses over
\emph{one} claim set whose relative ordering is known by construction and whose gold relation
graph is known, so scoring error separates from extraction error.

\subsection{Coherence ladders}

\begin{definition}[Coherence ladder]
\label{def-ladder}
A \emph{ladder} is a tuple $(\mathcal{P}, \langle \rho_0,\dots,\rho_4\rangle, \Gamma)$ where
$\mathcal{P}$ is a base \emph{relation plan} --- a claim set with a declared relation graph
--- each rung $\rho_k$ is a sequence of perturbation operator calls applied to $\mathcal{P}$
yielding a response and its own gold relation graph, and $\Gamma$ is a set of ordering
constraints over the rungs' scores. A ladder is \emph{admitted} only if all five rungs pass
every validation gate.
\end{definition}

All-or-nothing admission is forced by the design: four of the five rungs carry no standalone claim,
only one relative to their neighbours, so the ladder --- not the item --- is the unit of validity.

Ordering constraints come in three classes, asserted per readout: \textbf{C1}, strict increase
between consecutive rungs; \textbf{C2}, a \emph{predicted} decrease or invariance --- where
Proposition~\ref{prop-quirk}'s concession dip becomes a falsifiable prediction rather than an
excuse, and where an \emph{ordering-only} rung must be exactly flat since
Table~\ref{tab:compile} gives it no factor; and \textbf{C3}, endpoint separation between rungs
$0$ and $4$. A measure is scored on the fraction it satisfies.

\subsection{Generation}

Generation is a five-stage LLM pipeline with fixed, versioned prompts.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.9em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
\item[P1] \emph{Topic $\to$ question.} One open-ended question whose answer will naturally carry
  diverse logical structure. The prompt asks for adjudicative domains --- incident investigations,
  litigation, competing hypotheses --- which produce exhaustive alternatives and resolving
  holdings, and forbids the words \emph{cause}, \emph{condition}, \emph{alternative},
  \emph{contrast} so relations are not primed lexically.
\item[P2] \emph{Question $\to$ claims.} $26$ tagged atomic claims: $14$ correct, $4$
  plausible-but-false, an \emph{exhaustive alternative pair} of which exactly one must hold, a
  disjunctive pair, and two \emph{holding} claims able to resolve a tension.
\item[P3] \emph{Claims $\to$ relation plan.} Select $14$--$16$ claims, renumber to consecutive
  assertion positions, and plan $8$--$12$ relations over distinct pairs, each with a sense from
  Table~\ref{tab:compile}, a validity label and a strength band. At least two false claims must be
  selected, so factuality cannot be inferred from coherence.
\item[P4] \emph{Plan $\to$ response.} Prose realizing the plan exactly: assert each atom once, in
  position order, referring back by pronoun rather than restatement. Quantifiers, numbers, dates,
  polarity and modals are content, not wording.
\item[P5] \emph{Response $\to$ rung.} Apply one rung's perturbation calls, changing the minimum
  number of words (operators in Appendix~\ref{app-operators}).
\end{description}

\subsection{Validation}

Every threshold lives in one table and generation is rejected rather than repaired. An independent
\emph{committee}, distinct from the generator, runs three majority-gated validators. \textbf{V1}
is load-bearing: recover the relations the response \emph{itself} draws, admitting only if
$\ge0.80$ of the \emph{valid} planned relations come back with the correct coupling and $\ge0.70$
with the correct sense --- this certifies that the prose realizes the plan, so the gold graph
describes the response and not the plan's intention. \textbf{V3} audits the prose (fluency,
formality, organization each $\ge4$ of $5$; no span may reveal the text came from an annotation
plan) and \textbf{V4} requires every atom present. Plan structure is gated separately, including a
$0.55\pm0.15$ valid fraction and $\ge2$ false claims selected, so factuality cannot be inferred
from coherence. Appendix~\ref{app-gates} gives every threshold and two measurement subtleties: the
$4$--$6$ \emph{declared non-relations} per item, the only real negative set against which a false
positive is meaningful, and the separation of relation \emph{identity} (the unordered pair) from
the \emph{grading} of direction, coupling and sense.

\paragraph{Per-rung gold relations.} An earlier generator perturbed each rung's \emph{text} but
re-attached the base plan's relation graph to all five rungs. The gold relations were then constant
within a family, so every readout was constant too and the strict-increase constraints could not
hold by construction: the gold arm satisfied $2/28$. Deriving each rung's edges from its own
operator calls, and rejecting any family where two \emph{adjacent} rungs build the same edge set
while the higher rung's calls claim a change, raises this to $22/28$ on the same topics and
generator. This is the clearest evidence that the ladder tests a measure rather than the corpus ---
and a warning that a benchmark of this shape can silently be untestable.

\section{Evaluation protocol and baselines}
\label{sec-baselines}
%% ================================================================

An \emph{arm} fixes where the relation graph comes from, holding atoms, priors and readouts
identical: \textsc{gold} takes each item's own labelled relations, \textsc{gold\_valid} only those
labelled valid, and a \textsc{mined} arm runs the estimator of \S\ref{sec-mining} on the response
text. This is the central experimental device --- the gold arm is a ceiling isolating
\emph{scoring} error, so any shortfall below it is \emph{extraction} error. Mined relations are
compared to gold at three nested levels: \textbf{pair} (were these two atoms related at all),
\textbf{coupling} (pair, plus the Level-1 coupling, with direction required for asymmetric
couplings), and \textbf{sense}. The coupling level is the one that matters, being the level at
which the factor table is chosen.

A coherence measure should be compared against controls chosen to fail informatively. We propose
five families (Appendix~\ref{app-baselines}): \emph{ladder-blind controls} (atom count, relation
count, length, perturbation-call count), which must be flat on \textsc{control} and fail C3
elsewhere, and which catch the likeliest way such a measure is secretly wrong; \emph{local
contradiction detection}, pairwise NLI with no global model \citep{selfcheckgpt}, isolating what
joint inference adds; \emph{LLM judges} --- G-Eval's coherence dimension \citep{geval} and a direct
1--5 rating --- the practical incumbent, and a demanding test since rungs differ by a few words;
\emph{discourse representations}, DiscoScore \citep{discoscore} and an entity grid
\citep{barzilay}, strongest on \textsc{order} and weakest on \textsc{conflict}; and \emph{internal
ablations} over priors, readouts, pair policies, prompt variants and the gold ceiling. Beyond
ladder ordering we recommend Spearman correlation against human ratings: ordering agreement is the
sharper instrument, being a controlled contrast, but human correlation establishes that the
construct ordered is the one readers care about.

\paragraph{Relation extraction is the bottleneck.} Three findings from Table~\ref{tab:mining}.
\textsc{all\_pairs} achieves the best recall ($82.6\%$) and by far the worst precision
($9.3\%$): mining every pair finds the true relations and buries them, emitting $867$ edges against
$86$ gold, and since the readouts are products over relations this is what drives the marginals
toward the prior. The forward-only policies are \emph{structurally} capped --- backward recall
$23$--$27\%$ against $73.3\%$ --- because $20$ of the $86$ gold edges are both directed and
backward and no forward-only candidate set can emit them; admitting both arc directions within the
window recovers most of that gap at a fifth of the calls. And the two prompt revisions
(rebalancing the few-shot label distribution toward the true base rate, and requiring a verifiable
evidence span) raise coupling F1 from $35.5$ to $56.3$ while driving declared-non-relation
violations to zero and illegal-vocabulary emissions from $25$ to $0$. Precision, not recall, is
where a grounded miner is won.

\paragraph{The model orders correctly when the graph is correct.} On gold relations the readouts
satisfy $78.6\%$ of the declared constraints (Table~\ref{tab:mining}) --- $22$ of $28$, against
$2/28$ pre-fix --- while mined arms reach $35.7$--$53.6\%$. Since readouts, atoms and priors are
identical across arms, the $25$--$43$ point gap is attributable to extraction, consistent with
Table~\ref{tab:mining}: at $56.3\%$ coupling F1 roughly half the factors in a mined network are
wrong or missing, and a ladder whose rungs differ by one or two edges cannot survive that. Arm
ordering does not track F1 --- \textsc{gated} scores best among mined arms at the second-worst F1
--- which at $28$ assertions per arm we read as noise, and which is why the corpus must grow before
policies are ranked on ladder agreement.

\paragraph{Readout dynamic range is policy-dependent.} On nine hand-authored fixtures scored
across three models and three strength estimators, the readouts differ sharply in spread. Under the
response-grounded \textsc{windowed} policy the estimator asserts almost no contradiction edges, so
$\cons\equiv1.00$ and $\rei\equiv0.50$ (spread $0.00$) while $\mm$ (spread $0.87$) and $\lp$
(spread $1.00$) discriminate, separating a consistent biography ($\mm=0.99$) from the same
biography with five planted contradictions ($0.82$); under \textsc{all\_pairs} the same readouts
regain range ($\rei$ spans $0.00$--$0.50$). The degeneracy is thus a property of the \emph{graph},
not the readout: $\mathcal{K}=1.00$ is the \emph{correct} answer for a conflict-free response,
verified on an authored post-mortem ($\mm=0.939$, $\mathcal{K}=1.000$, $1.9$ relations per atom),
and is uninformative exactly when the estimator asserts no conflicts at all. Aggregated over all
cells, contradiction-heavy fixtures average $\mm=0.560$ against $0.767$ for conflict-free ones.
Hence reporting $\mm$ as headline with $\mathcal{K},\mathcal{S}$ as diagnostics: a number that is
$1.00$ both for a sound argument and an unmined one is not usable alone.

\section{Two worked examples}
\label{sec-examples}
%% ================================================================

\input{figures/graph-aeroparts}

The response is a $16$-atom aviation recall report. Its relations are $12$ support edges --- an
entailment spine $a_1\!\to\!\dots\!\to\!a_6$ (shipment, installation, service entry, in-flight
failures, probe, grounding order) with branches, plus one \coupling{equivalence} --- and two
conflicts. All values are exact, from $2^{16}$ enumeration.

\begin{example}[Incoherent]
\label{ex-incoherent}
The report asserts both that \emph{no passengers or crew were harmed} and that a wire report
\emph{claimed three passengers died}. These are exhaustive alternatives, not mere opposition:
they cannot both hold and cannot both fail, so the relation is \coupling{exclusive} with
$p=0.93$, and nothing in the response resolves it. A second tension --- the airline's
pilot-error argument against the report's metallurgical-defect finding, $p=0.80$ --- is a
\sense{Concession}, resolved by the tribunal holding $a_{13}$ and discounted by
Eq.~\ref{eq-concession}. The model returns $\LCS_{\mm}=0.607$ and $\log Z=-9.64$
(Figure~\ref{fig:aero-incoherent}); the marginal of the atom on the losing side of the
unresolved conflict is dragged \emph{below} its own prior, which is the per-atom diagnostic
that localizes the incoherence. Applying the concession discount alone raises the score to
$0.612$.
\end{example}

\begin{example}[Coherent]
\label{ex-coherent}
Removing the two conflict edges and changing nothing else leaves the identical support
structure (Figure~\ref{fig:aero-coherent}). The score rises to $\LCS_{\mm}=0.620$ with
$\log Z=-8.25$ and $\LCS_{\lp}=1.00$: the response is now as coherent as its own edge
skeleton allows, which is what the ceiling of Eq.~\ref{eq-logz} means. The suppressed atom
recovers to its prior.
\end{example}

Because the panels differ \emph{only} in the two conflict edges, the $0.607\!\to\!0.620$ lift and
the $1.39$ nats of recovered mass are attributable to conflict structure alone --- the property the
ladder construction of \S\ref{sec-locobench} generalizes. The full \textsc{conflict} ladder is
monotone throughout: $0.586 < 0.607 < 0.612 < 0.613 < 0.620$ over the rungs worse, base,
concession\_resolved, fix\_one\_conflict, coherent.\footnote{Treating
both tensions as plain \coupling{contradiction} rather than \coupling{exclusive} --- the
three-coupling model of \citet{factreasoner} --- gives $0.587$ and $\log Z=-9.75$ for
Example~\ref{ex-incoherent}, with Example~\ref{ex-coherent} unchanged at $0.620$ and
$-8.25$. The exhaustiveness distinction of \S\ref{sec-coherence} is worth $0.020$ here.}

A second fixture pair makes the order-sensitivity claim concretely: two summaries of one appellate
judgment from the \emph{same} facts, differing only in whether self-serving claims precede or
follow the findings that qualify them. The faithful ordering scores $\mm=0.95$, the
self-serving-first ordering $0.90$ --- and no per-atom check can separate them, every atom being
identical.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec-limitations}
%% ================================================================

The empirical scope is the main limitation and is stated in \S\ref{sec-experiments}: two
families, ten items, one \textsc{conflict} ladder type, and single-sample mining with measured
run-to-run movement of $0.06$ F1. The \textsc{chain}, \textsc{order} and \textsc{control} ladders
are specified and gate-checked but ungenerated, so the false-positive check \textsc{control}
provides --- arguably the most important single experiment in the protocol --- has not been run,
and the baselines of \S\ref{sec-baselines} are argued for rather than measured.

Three modelling limitations. The vocabulary is pairwise by construction, so genuinely $k$-ary
structure (three claims jointly inconsistent, none pairwise so) is outside the family; the
transitivity and double-conflict rules that would capture it are expressible only in the Markov
logic view of Appendix~\ref{app-mln}. The concession discount's $\lambda=0.45$ and the holding-cue
heuristic are the one place hand-set constants enter. And production inference is approximate while
the exact values reported come from enumeration ($n\le20$); the agreement of the two is not
characterized at length. Finally the corpus is LLM-generated throughout, gold labels included, with
LLM-committee validation: V1's $\ge0.80$ gate certifies the prose realizes the plan, but a
systematic bias shared between generator and committee would escape it, so human verification of a
sample is required before treating the corpus as ground truth.

\section{Conclusion}
%% ================================================================

Coherence is a property of a joint distribution over what a response asserts, and it can be read
off a Markov random field whose factors are the relations the response itself draws. Formalizing
the vocabulary as two levels --- interpretable discourse senses compiling deterministically onto
five inferential couplings, each fixed by the world it penalizes --- keeps annotation tractable
while holding the model to a closed pairwise family. Of four readouts, the mean posterior marginal
alone is monotone in conflict resolution, bounded, constant-free and per-atom diagnosable; two
plausible alternatives fail for a reason worth remembering, that a score asking whether asserted
relations are honoured is maximized by asserting nothing. Measuring such a score requires
controlled contrasts, and LoCoBench supplies them as ladders whose orderings are declared before
any measurement. Gold relations recover $78.6\%$ of those orderings, mined relations $53.6\%$;
since the arms differ only in the relation graph, the gap localizes the open problem --- the
probabilistic model is adequate and grounded relation extraction, at $56.3\%$ coupling-level F1, is
the bottleneck. That is a more tractable target than ``measure coherence'', and it is measurable on
a corpus we release.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}

This work involves no human subjects and no personally identifying data. The fixtures include
a summary of a published appellate judgment, used as a worked example of ordering-dependent
coherence; it is a matter of public record and no private individual is the subject of
analysis. A coherence measure can be misread as a truth measure: a response can be perfectly
coherent and entirely false, which is exactly why \S\ref{sec-twostage} keeps the two axes
separable, and we caution against deploying $\LCS$ alone as a factuality filter. The corpus
deliberately contains false claims, labelled as such, and should not be redistributed without
those labels.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}

\section{The reified coherence node}
\label{app-reified}
%% ================================================================

\paragraph{(c) Reified coherence node.} Add a binary variable $R$ with prior $\rho$ and one
ternary vote factor per relation,
\begin{equation}
h_r(R,a_s,a_t)=\begin{cases}
1 & R=0\\
1 & R=1,\ r \text{ satisfied at } (a_s,a_t)\\
1-p_r & R=1,\ r \text{ violated},
\end{cases}
\qquad \LCS_{\rei}=\pr{R=1}.
\label{eq-reified}
\end{equation}
\emph{Satisfied} is the complement of the penalized set of Def.~\ref{def-couplings}, so
\coupling{entailment} is satisfied everywhere but $(1,0)$ --- including vacuously at $a_s=0$.

\section{Atom alignment across stages}
\label{app-align}
%% ================================================================

\paragraph{Alignment.} Matching Stage-1 atoms to Stage-2 atoms is done by object identity
first (the reuse path, when atomization is shared), then by normalized text, and only then
by id. Text precedes id deliberately: near-duplicate removal keeps the first-seen atom and
leaves the surviving id set sparse, so two independent atomizations can disagree about which
claim lives at which index, and matching on id alone would attach a prior to the
\emph{wrong} atom --- worse than attaching none. When coverage falls below one half the run
is flagged degraded, and the configured policy is to warn, raise, or discard every prior and
fall back to a clean coherence-only score rather than a half-primed mixture.

\section{Candidate-pair policies}
\label{app-pairs}
%% ================================================================

\paragraph{Candidate pairs.} Mining all $n(n-1)$ ordered pairs is quadratic and, worse,
over-connects. We define four policies: \textsc{all\_pairs}; \textsc{windowed}, forward
pairs with $0<j-i\le w$ ($w=4$); \textsc{gated}, the window plus long-range forward pairs
passing a similarity gate; and \textsc{bidirectional}, both arc directions within
$|j-i|\le w$. The last exists because a forward-only policy \emph{cannot emit} a directed
arc that runs backward in atom order, so such gold relations are unreachable under it
regardless of the model --- a structural cap we measure in \S\ref{sec-experiments}. All
policies are refined against the response: pairs outside the window that the prose links by
shared entity or sentence adjacency are \emph{promoted}, and in-window pairs the prose does
not link are \emph{demoted}.

\section{Validation gates}
\label{app-gates}
%% ================================================================

Every acceptance threshold lives in one table, and generation is rejected rather than
repaired. An independent \emph{committee} of models --- distinct from the generator ---
runs three validators, each gating by majority:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{V1, relation recovery.} Given the response and its atoms, recover the
  relations the response \emph{itself} draws. Admission requires $\ge0.80$ of the
  \emph{valid} planned relations recovered with the correct coupling and $\ge0.70$ with the
  correct sense. This is the load-bearing gate: it certifies that the prose actually realizes
  the plan, so the gold graph describes the response rather than the plan's intention.
\item \textbf{V3, prose audit.} Fluency, formality and organization each $\ge4$ of $5$, with
  the \emph{leakage} and \emph{hedging} span lists empty --- no span may reveal that the text
  was generated from an annotation plan.
\item \textbf{V4, atom coverage.} Every atom present: coverage $=1.00$ over the statuses
  \emph{missing} and \emph{merged}. The status \emph{altered} is deliberately non-gating,
  being the judgment most sensitive to the surface rewording P4 licenses.
\end{itemize}

Plan structure is gated separately: $14$--$16$ claims, $8$--$12$ relations, $4$--$6$
declared \emph{non-relations}, a valid-relation fraction of $0.55\pm0.15$, and at least two
false claims selected.

\section{Proposed baselines in detail}
\label{app-baselines}
%% ================================================================

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \emph{Ladder-blind controls}: atom count, relation count, response length, and number
  of perturbation calls applied. These must be flat on \textsc{control} and must fail C3
  elsewhere. They cost nothing and they catch the most likely way a coherence measure is
  secretly wrong --- by tracking edit count or graph density.
\item \emph{Local contradiction detection}: pairwise NLI over atom pairs with no global
  model, scored as (one minus) the fraction of pairs labelled contradictory, in the spirit of
  SelfCheckGPT's NLI variant \citep{selfcheckgpt}. This is the ablation that isolates what
  joint inference adds over local detection, and it should be expected to do reasonably on
  \textsc{conflict} and poorly on \textsc{chain}, where the defect is a \emph{missing}
  entailment rather than a present contradiction.
\item \emph{LLM judges}: G-Eval's coherence dimension \citep{geval} and a direct
  ``rate the logical coherence from 1 to 5'' prompt. These are the practical incumbent and
  the comparison reviewers will want. The ladder is a demanding test for them because rungs
  differ by a few words, so a judge must be sensitive at very small edit distances while
  remaining flat on \textsc{control}.
\item \emph{Discourse representations}: DiscoScore \citep{discoscore}, and an entity-grid
  model \citep{barzilay} as the classical reference. Both are order-sensitive by
  construction, so \textsc{order} is where they should be strongest and \textsc{conflict},
  where the perturbation is semantic rather than positional, where they should be weakest.
\item \emph{Internal ablations}: uniform versus factuality priors (Eq.~\ref{eq-priors}); the
  four readouts of \S\ref{sec-readouts}; the four candidate-pair policies; the two prompt
  variants; and the gold-relation ceiling. These separate the model's own design choices from
  the estimator's accuracy.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Correspondence with Markov logic}
\label{app-mln}
%% ================================================================

The coherence MRF has a natural first-order encoding \citep{mln}, with
$\pr{\mathbf{a}}=\tfrac1Z\exp\bigl(\sum_k w_k n_k(\mathbf{a})\bigr)$ for $n_k$ the number of
true groundings of formula $F_k$. Evidence predicates $\mathit{Entail}(i,j)$,
$\mathit{Contradict}(i,j)$, $\mathit{Equiv}(i,j)$ and $\mathit{Resolves}(h,i,j)$ carry the
mined graph; $\mathit{Holds}(i)$ is the query predicate.

The naive encoding --- one weighted clause per relation, e.g.\
$\mathit{Entail}(i,j)\wedge a_i \Rightarrow a_j$ with $w=\mathrm{logit}(p)$ --- is
\textbf{not} equivalent to Definition~\ref{def-mrf}. Its factor
$[e^w,e^w,1,e^w]$ is not row-stochastic: it rewards the vacuous $a_s=0$ rows on both target
states. Brute force over $2^{16}$ worlds gives mean marginal support $0.455$ against the
MRF's $0.587$, with per-atom marginals differing by up to $0.31$ --- two different models,
not two encodings of one. Its mode is degenerate for the reason
Proposition~\ref{prop-vacuous} identifies: the all-false world satisfies every implication
vacuously, so the most coherent reading of any response is the empty one.

Expanding each relation into three clauses restores the correspondence. Writing
$\ln\psi(a_s,a_t)=\alpha+\beta a_s+\gamma a_t+\delta a_s a_t$, the conjunction weight is
exactly $\pm\mathrm{logit}(p)$ --- the interaction the implication was meant to carry --- and
the source unit clause is the correction restoring row-stochasticity:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
$\tau$ & $\alpha$ & $\beta$ & $\delta$ \\
\midrule
\coupling{entailment}    & $\ln\frac{1-p}{\pi_s}$ & $0$ & $+\mathrm{logit}(p)$ \\
\coupling{contradiction} & $\ln\frac{p}{\pi_s}$   & $0$ & $-\mathrm{logit}(p)$ \\
\coupling{equivalence}   & $\ln\frac{1-p}{p}$ & $\ln\frac{1-p}{p}$ & $+2\,\mathrm{logit}(p)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
With this expansion the ground Markov logic network reproduces the MRF exactly: mean support
$0.5866$ and $\log Z=-9.751$ for both, with zero per-atom difference to machine precision. The
expansion is established for the three original couplings; \coupling{exclusive} and
\coupling{co\_necessity} are not tabulated. The reason to prefer the MRF for the measure
itself is that its pairwise family is closed at three parameters, so the vocabulary cannot
quietly grow; the reason to keep the Markov logic view is that the beyond-pairwise rules
(transitivity, concession cancellation, double conflict) are expressible there and are the
natural route past the pairwise limitation noted in \S\ref{sec-limitations}.
